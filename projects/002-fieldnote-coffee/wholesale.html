The fictional programme includes recipe calibration and practical brew guidance. No formal certification or accredited training is claimed.
Do you offer custom blends? ＋
A custom blend brief could be explored once volumes, equipment and flavour preferences are understood. Any recipe, price and availability would need confirmation.
Where do you deliver? ＋
The fictional business is based in Cape Town. Delivery routes and minimums would be agreed case by case; no real courier agreement exists.
