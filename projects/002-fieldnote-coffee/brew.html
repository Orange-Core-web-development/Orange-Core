03 / THE GRIND NOTES

Think of grind
as a dial.

Grind size changes how quickly water moves through coffee and how much surface area it can reach. If a cup tastes thin or sharp, try a finer step. If it tastes heavy or dry, try a coarser step. Keep the dose and water the same while you test.

COARSEPlunger / French pressLonger immersion, slower extraction

04 / WATER NOTES

Most of your cup
is water.

Start with fresh, good-tasting water and keep it consistent while you dial in a recipe. Temperature, mineral content and kettle technique can all change the result. If your water tastes strongly of chlorine or has a heavy mineral character, compare it with a filtered option.

This is practical brewing guidance, not a water-quality specification. Follow the recommendations for your brewer and local water supply.
