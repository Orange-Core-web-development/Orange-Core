02 — THE MACHINEFORM / FUNCTION / FEELING

LESS NOISE.
MORE SIGNAL.

The A-01 is a fictional performance concept created to explore a digital product experience. Its surfaces, proportions and details are designed as one coherent expression: purposeful, restrained and unapologetically alive. The body is imagined around a compact electric architecture, with a low cabin, pronounced rear haunches and a clean uninterrupted shoulder line.

01 / THE SILHOUETTELOW. WIDE.
UNMISTAKABLE.

EXTERIOR / PROPORTION

A SHAPE WITH
INTENTION.

The wedge profile creates a visual sense of forward motion even at rest. A cab-forward canopy keeps the roofline tight, while the wide rear shoulders suggest stability and traction. The design study favours a few confident volumes over decorative complexity.

  • Low-set front volume and sculpted nose
  • Cab-forward glass canopy
  • Wide rear haunches and integrated aero surfaces
  • Contrasting technical lower body

INTERIOR / DRIVER ENVIRONMENT

EVERYTHING
IN REACH.
NOTHING IN THE WAY.

The cockpit concept is deliberately calm. A driver-focused display, tactile primary controls and a low visual horizon create a space that feels purposeful rather than overloaded. Digital interfaces should reveal complexity only when it is useful.

03 — ENGINEERING APPROACHCONCEPT SYSTEMS

THE WHOLE IS THE PERFORMANCE

BUILT AROUND
THE RESPONSE.

Every system is considered in relation to the driver. The A-01 is a fictional electric platform study; these descriptions explain design intent rather than verified engineering specifications.

01 / POWER DELIVERY

TORQUE, SHAPED

A dual-motor all-wheel-drive layout is imagined to distribute power across changing grip conditions, with selectable response profiles for road and track.

02 / CHASSIS

CALM UNDER LOAD

A low battery position, wide track and adaptive damping concept aim to make direction changes feel progressive and predictable.

03 / AERODYNAMICS

DESIGNED BY AIR

Underbody channels, a rear diffuser and carefully managed cooling paths form a visual language where airflow has a job to do.
