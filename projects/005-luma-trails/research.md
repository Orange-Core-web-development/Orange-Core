# Day 5 research — Livingstone boutique safari & experiences

**Checked:** 19 November 2026 · **Business:** Luma Trails (fictional portfolio concept)

## Niche and customers
Livingstone serves international and regional visitors planning Victoria Falls access, river activities, wildlife, culture, transfers and safari extensions. Design assumptions: short-stay travellers needing half-/full-day planning; couples and small groups seeking curated pacing; families who need clear activity intensity and safety details; adventure travellers booking specialist operators; guests extending into regional trips such as Chobe that require border logistics.

## Offerings and buying motivations
Local operator menus commonly include Falls tours, river cruises, wildlife drives, rafting, canoeing, scenic flights, cultural visits, transfers, accommodation and combined activity packages. Visitors seek convenience, reliable logistics, guide knowledge, safety transparency, clarity about inclusions, seasonal availability and easy comparisons.

## Market conventions and website opportunities
Experience cards with activity and duration; departure/location notes; inclusions and exclusions; inquiry forms with dates and guest count; itinerary builders; transfer, border and passport reminders; seasonality/weather/water-level notes; genuine operator credentials; written cancellation terms; responsible wildlife viewing and community-respect information.

## Trust and licensing
Zambia Tourism's licensing portal lists licensed tour operators, travel agents and activity operators. The Tourism and Hospitality Act 2015 includes registration/licensing requirements for tourism enterprises. The fictional site makes no licence or accreditation claim. A real operator must confirm its licensing class, guides, insurance, safety procedures and supplier arrangements before launch.

## Seasonality
Water-based activities near Victoria Falls are affected by river levels and seasonal conditions. Zambia Tourism's Devil's Pool/Angel's Pool guide describes access as dependent on water levels and professional operator arrangements. Never promise access or dates without current confirmation. Spray, trails and border timings vary too.

## Concept choices
Luma Trails differentiates with an unhurried editorial voice, illustrated experience cards, flexible sample itineraries, a practical field guide and local trip-brief builder. All contacts, rates, guest stories and schedules are invented. Cross-border trips are enquiry-only and require passport/visa, border, supplier and schedule confirmation.
