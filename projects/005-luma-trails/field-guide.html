FIELD NOTES / 03

Leave space
for the details.

A few practical notes to help you arrive curious, prepared and ready to take it all in.

01

When to visit

River and falls conditions change through the year. Higher water brings more spray; lower water can reveal different viewpoints. Confirm conditions before choosing activities.

Seasonal pool guidance ↗
02

Pack with purpose

Comfortable walking shoes, sun protection, a light rain layer, refillable water bottle and dry bag are useful starting points. A lens cloth helps around spray.

03

Move respectfully

Follow guide instructions, keep wildlife distance, stay on permitted paths, avoid feeding animals and leave no litter. Respect community photography preferences.

04

Know the details

Check passport validity, entry rules, park fees, operator licensing, pickup times and cancellation terms before paying. Cross-border excursions need extra planning.

QUESTIONS, BEFORE THE JOURNEY

Good to know.

Are activities available year-round?

Not all activities operate every season. Water levels, weather and operator safety assessment may affect access and schedules.

Do you guarantee wildlife sightings?

No. Wildlife is unpredictable. This concept promises thoughtful guiding, not sightings.

Can I combine experiences?

Yes, sample journeys can combine compatible activities. Final plans depend on time, transport, availability and conditions.

Are prices inclusive of park fees?

Sample prices are illustrative. A real quote would list fees, taxes, transfers, inclusions and exclusions.

What if weather changes our plan?

Activities may be adjusted, delayed or cancelled when conditions make them unsafe. A real operator should explain alternatives and refund/rescheduling terms.
