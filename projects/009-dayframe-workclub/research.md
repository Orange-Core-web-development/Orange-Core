# Day 9 research — Flexible workspace & coworking in Lusaka

**Research snapshot: 23 November 2026.** DAYFRAME is fictional. Market findings are sourced; brand, pricing, facilities, contact details, policies and visual direction are design assumptions.

## Niche overview
Flexible workspace businesses rent work-ready environments without a conventional long lease. Common offers range from day passes and hot desks to dedicated desks, private offices, virtual offices, meeting rooms, training rooms and event venues. Operators may also sell community programming, mail handling, printing, refreshments and office support.

## Customer types
- Freelancers, independent consultants and remote employees needing a desk away from home.
- Early-stage startups and small teams seeking affordable flexible space as headcount changes.
- Established companies or project teams needing temporary offices, meeting rooms or training venues.
- NGOs, consultants and visiting professionals needing a local work base.
- Facilitators and organizers who need room capacity and AV.

## Buying motivations and objections
Customers compare internet/power reliability, quietness, location and commute, access hours, desk availability, privacy, meeting-room availability, price clarity, amenities and community. Tours, trial passes, transparent inclusions and a simple booking path reduce uncertainty. Some visitors want connection; others need privacy and quiet, so community should not be compulsory.

## Market conventions observed
Local offers include hourly/day/week/month rates, hot desks, dedicated desks, private offices, meeting/boardrooms, training/event venues and virtual-office services. Internet, printing, beverages, parking, reception, mail handling, access hours, room credits, event programming and trial visits are common differentiators. These are examples of operators' offers, not category-wide guarantees.

## Trust signals and website features
- Real address, actual photos, current hours and contact channels.
- Clear room capacity, access policy, inclusions and booking/cancellation terms.
- Pricing or a clear enquiry path.
- Tour/day-pass CTA, floor plans and photo gallery.
- Space filters, membership comparison, room request form and event calendar.
- Accessibility and actual internet/power arrangements should be verified, not implied.
- Member quotes/logos must be authentic and permissioned; this concept uses fictional snapshots.
