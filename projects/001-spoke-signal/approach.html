HONEST ABOUT THE LIMITS

Good judgement
is part of the job.

Not every repair belongs in a mobile workshop. We would rather explain a limit early than promise a service we cannot safely complete.

01

E-bike electronicsMechanical checks only in this concept. Battery, motor, firmware and electronic fault diagnostics are not offered.

02

Specialist suspension workFork and shock internals require dedicated facilities and are referred to an appropriate specialist.

03

Parts compatibilityStandards differ. We check the exact component and confirm availability before ordering or fitting.

04

Unsafe conditionsIf a repair cannot be completed safely at the agreed location, work pauses and the options are explained.
