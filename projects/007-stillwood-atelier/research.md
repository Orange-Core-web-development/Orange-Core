# Day 7 — Furniture restoration & bespoke woodworking in Lusaka

Research date: 21 November 2026. Concept: STILLWOOD Furniture Atelier (fictional). Scope: restoration, refinishing/reworking and custom furniture in Lusaka.

## Customer segments and motivations
Households with damaged, inherited or sentimental pieces; homeowners/renters seeking custom dimensions or finishes; interior designers; hospitality and office operators; collectors and buyers who value local craft. These segments are hypotheses, not measured demand. Motivations may include preserving materials, extending useful life, fitting a room, matching an interior, or understanding repair cost before committing. Friction includes hidden damage, timber/finish choices, quote scope, lead time and delivery.

## Services observed
Local woodworking businesses publicly present solid-wood and made-to-order furniture, beds, tables, chairs, wardrobes, kitchen units, doors/windows, accessories, installation, upholstery, repair and commercial/hospitality furnishing. A restoration atelier can distinguish itself with condition assessment, preservation-first repair, finish renewal, and written scope before work begins.

## Trust and useful website features
- Before/after photos with process captions and authentic project evidence.
- Itemised scope, quote, change approval, deposit and cancellation terms.
- Finish/material guides, care notes, realistic lead-time ranges and collection/delivery terms.
- Enquiry form requesting photos and approximate dimensions; no fake booking/payment flow.
- Testimonials only when genuine; this demo labels all stories as fictional.

## Competitor/reference observations
- [Kubu Crafts](https://kubucrafts.com/) describes handcrafted hardwood furniture, named wood types, customization, workshop/store locations and craft heritage; it states it has a Livingstone workshop-store and Lusaka stores.
- [Mupapa Wood](https://www.mupapawood.com/) describes solid-wood furniture using locally sourced Zambian hardwood and also presents architectural fittings and accessories.
- [Singogo Roberto’s Furniture](https://robertofurnitures.com/get-in-touch/) indexed listing includes custom furniture, upholstery, sofa manufacture, wardrobes, kitchen cabinetry and bedroom furniture. Direct page retrieval timed out, so the indexed listing is a lead only.
- [FixConnect carpentry](https://www.fixconnect.app/services/carpentry) shows service listings, repair, kitchen/wardrobe work, provider profiles, a job/quote pathway and visible price/payment information.
- [Rustic Pallets — Zambia MSME Spotlight, Ministry of Small and Medium Enterprise Development (March 2024 PDF)](https://www.msme.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/MSME-SPOTLIGHT-Magazine.pdf) documents a local furniture enterprise using pallet reuse, timber offcuts and various species. Sustainability statements are attributed to the enterprise, not generalized across the sector.
- [Mupapa Wood — Zambia Proudly Zambian Catalogue (2023 PDF)](https://www.mcti.gov.zm/pzc/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/PZC-Catalogue-3rd-edition-2023.pdf) is a government-hosted catalogue reference for locally produced wood furniture.

## STILLWOOD assumptions
Small appointment-based atelier rather than a high-volume retailer. Five pages: Home, Services, Process, Selected Work and Commission. Sample ZMW prices are illustrative. Service area, contact details, policies, project examples and testimonials are invented; no real credentials, clients, certifications or capacity are claimed. Design: bone, deep green, clay/rust, walnut and muted olive; original SVGs, system fonts, native JS filters and local enquiry brief generator.

## Limitations
Qualitative public-page research does not establish market size, average restoration prices, margins, demand or competitor capacity. Directories/indexed pages may be stale. Timber sourcing/species legality and supply must be verified for any real business.
