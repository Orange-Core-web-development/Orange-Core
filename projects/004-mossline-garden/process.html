04 / OUR APPROACH

Observe.
Shape. Grow.

A good outcome comes from a good sequence. We keep the decisions visible, the scope understandable and the handover useful.

PLACE✳PEOPLE✳PLANTS
01

LISTEN + LOOK

Understand the place.

We begin with the brief: how the space is used, what needs to change and what must stay. A site review records light, levels, access, existing trees, drainage, water and maintenance realities.

OUTPUT / SITE NOTES + PRIORITIES
The best idea
starts with
observation.
02

COMPOSE + COST

Make the plan legible.

We develop a spatial concept, planting direction, material palette and phased scope. Any estimate should identify assumptions, exclusions, allowances and items that need specialist pricing.

OUTPUT / CONCEPT + SCOPE
Clear decisions.
Fewer surprises.
Better work.
03

BUILD + HAND OVER

Bring the details together.

Once a scope is approved, the installation is coordinated around access, sequencing, material delivery and site protection. Handover notes explain what was installed and what needs attention next.

OUTPUT / COMPLETED SCOPE + HANDOVER
A garden is
made on site,
not on paper.
04

CARE + ADAPT

Let the garden mature.

Establishment care, pruning, mulching and watering routines are adjusted to the plants and the season. A garden is a living system, so the care plan is reviewed rather than left on autopilot.
