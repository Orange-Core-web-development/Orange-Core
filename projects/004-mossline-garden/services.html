GOOD TO KNOW

Before we begin.

Do you work with an existing gardener?

The concept service can include a handover plan and practical care notes for an existing gardener. Any training or supervision scope would be agreed in writing.

Can you work with a small courtyard?

Yes. The fictional studio is designed to handle compact courtyards, balconies, entry gardens and larger residential plots. The scope is tailored to access, light and intended use.

Do you install irrigation?

Irrigation can be included as a coordinated scope. A qualified irrigation specialist should verify water pressure, hydraulics, equipment and installation requirements before work begins.

How are plants selected?

Plant choices should respond to sun, shade, soil, drainage, water availability, mature size, maintenance capacity and local availability. A site-specific horticultural review is essential.
