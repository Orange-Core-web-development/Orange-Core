03 / STUDIO NOTES

Good to know
before you go.

01

Wear the mess

Clay can find your sleeves. Wear comfortable clothes you don't mind getting dirty, closed-toe shoes and tie back long hair.

02

Everything's here

Clay, basic tools, aprons and instruction are included in guided workshops unless a session description says otherwise.

03

Firing takes time

Pieces need to dry, be glazed and fired. Collection is usually 3–4 weeks after class; we'll message you when ready.

04

Shared studio

We clean tools, wash hands, keep food away from clay work and leave the bench ready for the next maker.

04 / THE FAQ

Small questions.
Clear answers.

Do I need experience?

No. First-spin, hand-building and colour-play sessions are designed for beginners. The guide demonstrates each technique before you start.

What happens to my pieces?

Pieces dry, receive a first firing, glaze and a second firing where appropriate. Collection typically takes 3–4 weeks. This is a fictional policy and timeframe.

Can I take my work home on the day?

Air-dry and unfired clay work is fragile and may not be durable. Workshop pieces are kept for finishing, then you receive a collection notice.

Can I book for a group?

Yes, the fictional studio concept accommodates small private groups. Enquire with your date, group size and preferred activity.

Is the studio accessible?

Access details would be confirmed before a real booking. The concept invites guests to contact the studio to discuss mobility, seating and other access needs in advance.

What is the cancellation policy?

Sample policy: reschedule with at least 48 hours' notice where a replacement slot is available. Late cancellations may forfeit the session credit. Confirm terms before paying.
