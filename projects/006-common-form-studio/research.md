# Day 6 research — COMMON FORM Clay Studio

**Date:** 20 November 2026  
**Niche:** Ceramics workshops, community pottery studios, creative classes and memberships in Lusaka, Zambia.

## Niche and business context
Pottery/ceramics experiences sell a mix of creative instruction, finished products, social experiences and access to studio tools. They can serve beginners wanting a single guided activity, returning hobbyists seeking skill progression, and groups booking a shared event. Lusaka has existing pottery experiences and ceramics retail, so this concept is grounded in a local market.

## Customer segments
- First-timers: want a friendly, low-pressure introduction and clear instructions.
- Hobby makers: want recurring sessions, skill progression and access to tools.
- Couples and friends: want a memorable shared activity.
- Families: need age guidance, supervision and predictable session structure.
- Corporate/private groups: need coordination, group size, pricing and timing.
- Gift buyers: want a simple experience or voucher concept.

## Common formats and products
Wheel throwing tasters and multi-session courses; hand-building, sculpture and decorative painting; group/private sessions, mobile workshops and adult-child sessions; finished ceramics retail; glazing, firing and collection workflows; studio memberships, open-studio access and kiln services in broader studio markets.

## Buying motivations
The concept is designed around creative expression, social outings, learning a tactile skill, taking a handmade object home, gifting an experience and returning to a studio community. These are design assumptions informed by observed service formats, not quantified consumer-survey findings.

## Market conventions and useful website features
- Show session length, skill level, group capacity and materials included.
- Explain drying, glazing, firing and collection.
- Give sample pricing and disclose extras.
- Provide enquiry/booking, group-event details, gift options, cancellation policies and FAQs.
- Communicate what to wear, safety rules, age limits, accessibility and photo consent.
- Label fictional testimonials and studio stories clearly.

## Trust signals
For a real studio: instructor experience, actual student work, workshop photos, transparent booking terms, real reviews, clear firing/collection expectations, hygiene/safety procedures, access information and a working contact method. Do not invent qualifications or customer reviews.

## Fictional concept
**COMMON FORM Clay Studio** is a welcoming, design-led clay club in Lusaka for beginners, hobby makers, friends, families and private groups. It offers wheel throwing, hand-building, short courses, glaze exploration, group sessions and sample studio credits.

## Website map
- Home: positioning, featured workshops and illustrative testimonial.
- Workshops: six formats and client-side filters.
- Studio credits: sample plans and interactive cost comparison.
- Studio: practical notes, care and accessible FAQs.
- Booking: enquiry brief builder with local copy-to-clipboard.
