THE PROJECT PATH

Four steps.
No mystery.

Each stage should leave you with a clearer understanding of the next decision.

01

DISCOVERY

Listen before we specify.

We discuss your property, current supply, outage experience, priorities, budget range and the appliances that matter. For a business, that includes opening hours, production schedules and critical equipment.

YOU LEAVE WITHA written brief of the problem to solve and the information still needed.
02

SITE & LOAD REVIEW

Measure the real conditions.

A real project would require an appropriate site visit and competent technical assessment: load measurements, roof or ground conditions, shading, cable routes, distribution board, earthing, access and equipment location.

YOU LEAVE WITHA site record, constraints list and a confirmed load picture.
03

SYSTEM DESIGN

Make the trade-offs visible.

The design should explain generation, storage, inverter capacity, critical circuits, protection, operating modes, expected limitations and a realistic path for future expansion. Savings and payback should be modelled from verified inputs, not guessed.

YOU LEAVE WITHA documented proposal, scope, assumptions, exclusions and quote.
04

INSTALL & HANDOVER

Finish with understanding.

A real installation would be completed by appropriately authorised and competent personnel, tested and documented. Handover should cover safe operation, shutdown, monitoring, maintenance, warranty paperwork and who to contact if something changes.

YOU LEAVE WITHTest records, labels, system documentation and a practical user walkthrough.

THE NON-NEGOTIABLES

Safety is part
of the design.

Electrical work and grid interaction are not DIY guesswork. A genuine provider must confirm the applicable Zambian requirements, authorisations, standards and utility approvals for the actual scope.

01 / AUTHORISATION

Check the licence.

Verify that the business holds the current ERB authorisation required for the activities it undertakes. Never rely on a logo or a vague claim.

GOOD QUESTIONS TO ASK ANY INSTALLER

Before you
sign anything.

Who will actually design and install the system?+

Ask for the responsible technical person's name, qualifications, registration where required and the company's relevant ERB authorisation. Verify the details independently.

What exactly is included in the quote?+

Request a component schedule, protection equipment, mounting, cabling, labour, testing, commissioning, transport, exclusions and any taxes or fees. Make sure the scope matches the site.

How long will the battery run my appliances?+

Ask for the assumptions: usable capacity, selected circuits, actual loads, inverter losses, battery limits, temperature and expected operating conditions. A single headline runtime is not enough.

What happens if something fails?+

Get the manufacturer warranty documents, installer workmanship terms, service contact, response process and any exclusions in writing.
