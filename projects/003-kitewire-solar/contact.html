COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we
get into it.

Can you promise a monthly saving?+

No responsible estimate should promise savings without verified consumption, tariff, system performance, operating assumptions, financing and maintenance costs. This concept makes no savings claim.

Can a solar system export power to the grid?+

ZESCO operates a net-metering application process. Eligibility, approved equipment, technical requirements and utility approval must be confirmed for the actual property and installation.

Do I need a battery?+

It depends on your objectives, load timing, grid connection and budget. Solar generation and backup storage solve related but different problems; the design should explain the trade-off.
