READ THIS BEFORE YOU SIZE ANYTHING

A calculator is
not a site survey.

This tool is intentionally a conversation starter. It does not account for every load, surge current, battery chemistry, inverter efficiency, solar resource, shading, roof strength, cable length, temperature, safety requirements or utility approval.
